## 2. Key Decisions & Rationales

1. **No CLA Re-signing Required:** Clarifications to `legal/CLA.md` are added to the explanatory preamble, Purpose, and Section *Why A CLA Alongside AGPL-3.0-Or-Later?* as a binding Project Steward Governance Policy/Covenant. The underlying grant of rights and signing phrase remain v1.0, preserving all existing recorded signatures without disruption.
2. **Strict Anti-SaaS Covenant:** Alternative terms offered by the Project Steward under the CLA are restricted to private on-premise/VPC internal operations (for companies with strict internal AGPL bans) or humanitarian/educational $0 waivers. No third-party commercial entity may receive a license to offer ISLAMU Event as a closed-source SaaS.
3. **Three-Pillar Architecture:** Transparently published in `README.md` to build trust:
   - Pillar 1: Public Commons (AGPLv3) - 100% Free & Open Source; all SaaS operators must publish source modifications.
   - Pillar 2: Enterprise Internal-Use License - For private VPCs/intranets; strictly no external SaaS.
   - Pillar 3: Official Partner Program - Commercial activity via quality seal, branding, and services on 100% AGPL codebase.

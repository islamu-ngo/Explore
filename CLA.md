# ISLAMU Event Contributor License Agreement

The full Contributor License Agreement text is maintained at [`legal/CLA.md`](legal/CLA.md).

Every non-bot contributor must sign the CLA before a pull request can be merged.

## Quick Reference: How to Sign

When you open a pull request, the CLA Assistant bot will post instructions. Post a new comment with the exact text:

```text
I have read and agree to the ISLAMU Event Contributor License Agreement v1.0, and I confirm that I have the right to submit my contribution under it.
```

To re-run the CLA check after signing, post a comment containing only `recheck`.

## Why a CLA?

ISLAMU Event is distributed publicly under AGPL-3.0-or-later. The CLA grants the ISLAMU project steward additional inbound rights from contributors so the nonprofit can also offer ISLAMU Event under alternative terms for sustainability, enterprise, nonprofit, humanitarian, public-sector, or hosted-service deployments — without taking ownership away from contributors. See [`legal/CLA.md`](legal/CLA.md) for the full legal text.

# ISLAMU Event Case Patterns

These patterns are illustrative. They show traceability between principles, heuristics, and design decisions. They do not show measured stakeholder experience, production reliability, scholar approval, market viability, or long-term community impact.

## Strategic Patterns

- Islamic curation boundary and anti-scam posture.
- Instance-safety and ethical ticketing/payment direction.
- Privacy-oriented strategy.
- Federation and portability direction.
- Client/instance choice.
- Prayer-relative scheduling.
- Two-tier verification.

## Technical Patterns

- .NET, ASP.NET Core, Blazor/MudBlazor, PostgreSQL/EF Core, Keycloak, Cerbos/local authorization, Serilog/OpenTelemetry, Docker Compose/Aspire, TUnit/bUnit/integration/architecture tests.
- Keycloak/BFF token boundary.
- Runtime-switchable Cerbos/local authorization.
- HAL/HATEOAS authorization-aware affordances.
- EF Core tenant filters and trusted tenant resolution.
- PII-split modeling and contact-sharing consent.
- API-key hashing, idempotency, rate limiting, and upload-safety boundaries.

## UX Patterns

- Reject dark patterns.
- Use neutral free-plan labels.
- Make subscription and cancellation clarity comparable.
- Use evidence-based availability messaging.
- Avoid preselected paid or privacy-invasive options.
- Disclose fees before commitment.
- Show ticketing breakdown: organizer price, processing fee, platform fee/tax, refund recipient.
- Keep post-event engagement optional and proportionate.
- Treat accessibility/localization as requirements, not polish.

## Business Model Patterns

- Donations, clearly labeled sponsorships, mission-compatible grants, and transparent support.
- Reject selling user data, surveillance ads, hidden fees, artificial scarcity, financing/sponsor dependency, and hidden influence.
- GNU AGPLv3 community self-hosting and possible third-party managed hosting/SaaS with governance boundaries.
- Separate upstream nonprofit steward from commercial hosting providers as a hypothesis to reduce enshittification risk, not a guarantee.

## How To Use The Case

Use it as an example of implementation traceability and report wording. Do not use it as proof that I-VSD is validated, that users experience the system as ethical, or that the product has religious/legal certification.
